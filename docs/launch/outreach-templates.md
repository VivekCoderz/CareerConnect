# Employer outreach templates (J01, J02)

**Target:** 50+ approved employers and 200+ real listings by 10 Oct. **Each person sends 10 personal messages a day.**

Rules:

- **Personalise the first line** (company name, a role they're hiring for, or something recent they did). Copy-paste blasts get ignored.
- Never promise numbers we don't have yet. Say "launching 11 Oct", not "10,000 students".
- Log every contact in the team outreach sheet: company, person, channel, date, reply, and next follow-up.
- Follow up once after 3 days, then stop.

## 1. LinkedIn message (to an HR, founder or talent person)

> Hi [Name], I saw [Company] is hiring for [role / "your team is growing"]. I'm Ram from CareerConnect, a new job portal for students, freshers and early professionals that launches on 11 Oct.
>
> We're inviting a small group of **Founding Employers**: job posts are **free for life**, you get a verified badge, and you can manage applicants, interviews and offers in one dashboard.
>
> Could I post your current openings for you? Just send me the JD and I'll set it up. It takes 2 minutes of your time.

## 2. WhatsApp message (warm contact, alumni, a referral)

> Hi [Name] 👋 Ram here from CareerConnect.
