Rules for job posts in channels:
- Only **approved** listings from the site, with the site link (never a WhatsApp number to "apply here").
- Never post a job that asks candidates for money. If in doubt, check with the moderation SOP.
- Feed jobs (from public boards) can be posted, but say "via [source]".

### 3.4 Daily job post format (channels)

## 4. College ambassadors

- **Who:** 1–2 active students per department, recommended by faculty or the placement cell.
- **What they do:** share the launch post in their class groups on their batch's day, collect questions and bugs, and send them to Tripti.
- **What they get:** a certificate of appreciation and a LinkedIn recommendation from the team (no money, no fake promises).
- **Brief them (15 min call, 9 Oct):** what the site does, the no-fee rule, how to sign up with Google, where to report bugs, and their batch date.
